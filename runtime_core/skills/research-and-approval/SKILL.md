# Skill: Research And Approval

Use when the task touches current docs, architecture, risk, or approval-sensitive behavior.

## Rules
- Use bounded local and approved external research only when the task warrants it.
- Keep research evidence compact and source-linked.
- Preserve explicit human approval and clarification pauses.
- Do not convert resumable human-interrupt states into fake terminal success or generic blocked outcomes.
- Keep operator-facing summaries short; keep detailed reasoning in state, logs, or evidence.
